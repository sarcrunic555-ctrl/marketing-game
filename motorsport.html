BMW M Motorsport.

Striving for success in motor sport is intrinsic to the DNA of BMW M.

The world of motor sports is an exciting one, characterized by pure action, pulsing adrenaline and unconditional passion. The BMW M Motorsport racing programme and numerous outings in worldwide motorsport teams around the new BMW M Hybrid V8, the new BMW M4 GT3, the BMW M4 GT4 and the BMW M2 CS Racing embody the essence of BMW M: maximum dynamics and the highest level of engineering from Munich. Every day, here in the home of BMW M Motorsport, the engineers work on the continuation of that success story.

BECOME PART OF THE TEAM.

BMW M Motorsport: High performance and excellent service. Are you a motorsport enthusiast or already active in motorsport? Then you too can become part of the BMW M Motorsport family! With the automobiles from BMW M Motorsport, both beginners and competitive drivers can successfully compete in races all over the world. The teams and drivers additionally benefit from the excellent customer service, which ranges from parts supply to on-site support at the race track.

WE ARE ONE TEAM.

BMW M SPORTS TROPHY.

The BMW M Sports Trophy supports private drivers and teams who represent the BMW brand worldwide in various series and at renowned racing events. Every top ten result with BMW racing cars at selected events earns points for the overall ranking. The 25 best-placed drivers and the ten most successful teams receive attractive prize money.

BMW M Motorsport will continue to support private drivers and teams in the BMW Sports Trophy competitions in 2023. Participation is free of charge.

BMW M2 CUP & M2 CS RACING CUP.

Attractive series for private drivers and professionals - sign up now! With the BMW M2 CS Racing, BMW M Motorsport has been continuing its long tradition in entry-level racing while moving up a gear at the same time. As was the case with both predecessors, the BMW M235i Racing and BMW M240i Racing, the BMW M2 CS Racing continues to provide ambitious teams and drivers with an affordable opportunity to pursue their passion for motor racing, while helping talented young drivers and amateurs to take their driving skills to the next level at the wheel of a real race car.

MBEDDED. THE BMW M MOTORSPORT REAL-TIME VIDEO DOCUMENTARY.

The BMW M Motorsport real-time video documentary ‘MBEDDED’ shares exclusive behind-the-scenes footage of racing programmes, gets up close and personal with those in charge, and gives viewers a totally new perspective of how major decisions are made. The focus is on the entry into the LMDh class of the IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar Championship in 2023, with fascinating insights into the development of the high-tech car and preparations ahead of the first racing highlight, the 24 Hours of Daytona in January 2023. ‘MBEDDED’ also presents other main elements of the BMW M Motorsport programme, is there at all the major races with the BMW M4 GT3, and shows the drivers and team members in very personal moments. This page provides an overview of all the episodes of ‘MBEDDED’ on the BMW M Motorsport YouTube channel.
